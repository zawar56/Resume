About

About

I am a Strategic Studies scholar at the National Defence University, Islamabad, with a research focus on nuclear deterrence, strategic stability, South Asian security, foreign policy, and international relations.

I am particularly interested in understanding how security challenges evolve in an increasingly complex geopolitical landscape and how evidence-based research can contribute to effective policymaking.

My work explores both traditional and emerging security challenges, including nuclear strategy, India–Pakistan relations, the Kashmir dispute, narrative and hybrid warfare, regional geopolitics, human security, and governance.

My research and policy analyses have been published across national and international platforms, contributing to conversations on strategic affairs, regional security, and public policy.

To complement my academic research with practical policy experience, I have gained experience with government institutions and leading policy research organisations. These experiences have strengthened my understanding of legislative processes, governance, and the realities of policymaking beyond the classroom.

Beyond research, I have had the privilege of delivering a statement at the 61st Session of the United Nations Human Rights Council, representing youth perspectives at the World Bank Group Youth Summit, and securing 3rd Place in a National Research Poster Competition as the only undergraduate competing alongside postgraduate researchers.

My long-term goal is to contribute to policy research and strategic analysis that informs decision-making on security, defence, and governance in South Asia.

I welcome opportunities to collaborate with researchers, policymakers, think tanks, government institutions, and international organisations working in strategic studies, defence and security, foreign policy, international relations, and governance.
