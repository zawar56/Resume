01 — ABOUT

About

I am a Strategic Studies scholar at the National Defence University, Islamabad, with a research focus on nuclear deterrence, strategic stability, South Asian security, foreign policy, and international relations.

My work explores both traditional and emerging security challenges, including nuclear strategy, India–Pakistan relations, the Kashmir dispute, narrative and hybrid warfare, regional geopolitics, human security, and governance.

My research and policy analyses have been published across national and international platforms, contributing to conversations on strategic affairs, regional security, and public policy.

I have also gained practical policy exposure through internships and research experiences with government institutions and leading policy research organizations.

My long-term goal is to contribute to policy research and strategic analysis that informs decision-making on security, defence, foreign policy, and governance in South Asia.
