Strategic Studies Researcher

I am a Strategic Studies scholar at the National Defence University, Islamabad, with a research focus on nuclear deterrence, strategic stability, South Asian security, foreign policy and international relations.

Research & Policy Analysis

My work explores nuclear strategy, India–Pakistan relations, the Kashmir dispute, narrative and hybrid warfare, regional geopolitics, human security and governance.

Practical Policy Experience

My academic work is complemented by practical exposure to policy research, legislative processes, governance and institutional decision-making through internships and research opportunities.

Long-Term Vision

My goal is to contribute to policy research and strategic analysis that informs decision-making on security, defence and governance in South Asia while bridging the gap between academia and practice.
