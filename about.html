About Me

I am a Strategic Studies scholar at the National Defence University, Islamabad, with a research focus on nuclear deterrence, strategic stability, South Asian security, foreign policy, and international relations...

My work explores both traditional and emerging security challenges, including nuclear strategy, India–Pakistan relations, the Kashmir dispute, narrative and hybrid warfare, regional geopolitics, human security, and governance.

Beyond research, I have delivered a statement at the 61st Session of the United Nations Human Rights Council, represented youth perspectives at the World Bank Group Youth Summit, and secured 3rd Place in a National Research Poster Competition.
